## 💡 Tips for Better RAW Editing

### Shoot in RAW
If you're not already shooting in RAW, now's the time to switch. Check your camera's settings menu and change the image quality from JPEG to RAW. This gives you so much more flexibility when editing.

### Underexpose slightly
When shooting in RAW, it's better to slightly underexpose (make the image darker) than overexpose. You can easily recover detail in shadows, but blown-out highlights are lost forever.

### Use the histogram
The histogram in rawmakase shows you the tonal range of your image. Aim to have the graph spread evenly across the chart without touching the edges too much.

### Take breaks
Eye strain can affect your editing decisions. Take regular breaks to rest your eyes and come back with fresh perspective.
